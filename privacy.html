Privacy & limitations

Version: October 30, 2026. Applies to the current prototype and public validator directory.

What this version does

The monitoring dashboard and notification settings are simulated. The validator directory retrieves public information. StakeSafe does not hold funds, request private keys, sign transactions, or guarantee returns, validator performance, or security.

Local storage

Your theme choice, demo scenario, and alert preferences are stored in your browser. Reset demo clears the scenario and alert preferences. Clear this site's browser storage to remove all preferences, including the theme.

Requests to other services

Opening Validators requests public directory data from validators-api-main.je-cf9.workers.dev, the mainnet deployment linked by the Nimiq validators-api project. Its operator can receive connection metadata such as your IP address. Search and filtering happen locally after download. StakeSafe does not send wallet addresses or search queries to this API.

Hosting & external navigation

The hosting provider processes normal web requests and may retain operational logs according to its policies. This app adds no analytics, advertising trackers, or marketing cookies. External links take you to separately operated services with their own policies. Clipboard access is only requested when you click Copy validator address.

Your decision, in the official wallet

Validator names, descriptions, fees, and payout terms may be incomplete, stale, or supplied by operators. A listing is not an endorsement. Verify status and terms at wallet.nimiq.com before staking. Never send NIM directly to a validator address. Links do not preselect a validator or initiate a transaction.

Not an official Nimiq service

StakeSafe is an independent project, not an official Nimiq product. No affiliation, approval, or guarantee is implied. No external wallet, hardware device, or notification delivery has been verified in this prototype.

Questions and security reports

No contact details are collected in this version. A public issue tracker and private security reporting contact must be configured before release. Never post keys, seed phrases, personal data, or sensitive security details publicly.
