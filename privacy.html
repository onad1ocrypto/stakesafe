Privacy & limitations

Version: October 30, 2026. Applies to read-only mainnet checks and the public validator directory.

Read-only wallet integration

StakeSafe uses the official Nimiq Mini App SDK to request shared account addresses after you choose Connect Nimiq Pay. Account sharing requires wallet approval and does not prove ownership. No signatures, payments, private keys, seed phrases, or device identifiers are requested. Alternatively, you can enter a public mainnet address.

Consent and data sources

Before monitoring, you must agree to query your selected address through rpc.nimiqwatch.com. Requests contain the monitored address and its delegated validator address; the RPC operator can see these and connection metadata such as your IP address. Wallet balance may be read through Nimiq Pay on supported hosts, otherwise through the public RPC. Shared accounts other than the one you select are not queried. Directory data is fetched from validators-api-main.je-cf9.workers.dev; its operator receives normal connection metadata. Directory search and filtering are local.

Data retention

Monitored addresses, shared account lists, balances, and up to 100 check events stay in tab memory only. Disconnect or reload clears them. Theme and on-screen alert preferences are saved in browser storage. Clear this site's browser storage to remove preferences. No seed phrases, contact details, or wallet records are stored by a StakeSafe backend. No tracking cookies or analytics are added.

Foreground checks, not background alerts

While this page is visible, automatic checks run every 60 seconds after you connect, until paused or disconnected. On-screen notifications cannot reach you when the app is closed. No email, Telegram, push delivery, reward guarantees, or transaction execution are offered. Failed checks are reported; old balances are labeled last known rather than replaced with zero.

Hosting & external navigation

The hosting provider processes normal web requests and may retain operational logs according to its policies. This app adds no analytics, advertising trackers, or marketing cookies. External links take you to separately operated services with their own policies. Clipboard access is only requested when you click Copy validator address.

Your decision, in the official wallet

Validator names, descriptions, fees, and payout terms may be incomplete, stale, or supplied by operators. A listing is not an endorsement. Verify status and terms at wallet.nimiq.com before staking. Never send NIM directly to a validator address. Links do not preselect a validator or initiate a transaction.

Not an official Nimiq service

StakeSafe is an independent project, not an official Nimiq product. No affiliation, approval, or guarantee is implied. Read-only provider behavior is tested with simulated host responses. Physical-device compatibility must also be verified in Nimiq Pay; no background notification service is implemented.

Questions and security reports

Public issue tracker: onad1ocrypto/stakesafe/issues. GitHub private vulnerability reporting still needs to be enabled by the repository owner. Never post keys, seed phrases, personal data, or sensitive security details publicly.
